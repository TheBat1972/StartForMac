## Features

- **Apps by category.** Every app on your Mac is sorted into categories, like the Start menu in Windows. You can create your own categories and drag apps into them. The window opens from the Dock, with a hotkey (`⌃⌥P` by default) or with a trackpad gesture.
- **Search.** One field searches apps, files and folders, including text inside files and iCloud Drive. A calculator, unit conversion and currency conversion work here too, for example “15% of 8400”, “5 miles in km”, “100 usd in rub”. A file from the results can be dragged into any app or Finder (a copy is sent), and its context menu has “Copy file”, “Copy path” and “Show in Finder”. The window can be moved and resized, `Esc` closes it.
- **Custom icons.** Right-click an app or website → “Change icon…”: a ready-made set of icons or a picture from your disk. The app itself is not changed, “Restore the original icon” undoes the replacement.
- **Uninstall apps.** Right-click → “Uninstall…”: leftovers (caches, settings, logs, login items) are found, you review the list and confirm, and everything goes to the Trash. System files need your administrator password.
- **“Recent” tab.** The latest documents from any app, by day: “Today”, “Yesterday”, “This week”, “Earlier”. The count (25, 50, 100 or 200) is chosen on the tab.
- **“Sites” tab.** Quick links with icons, and folders. Order: “Manual”, “A–Z” or “Frequent”.
- **Colored category icons**, with your own icon and color for every custom category (Settings → “Icons”).
- **Sidebar buttons.** “Minimize all windows” minimizes every app's windows, “Lock screen” locks the screen at once.
- **Quick launch.** Five places at the bottom of the panel for your favorite apps.

### Tools (the key button)

Each tool opens in its own window and works independently of the main window.

- **Finder tweaks** (off by default). “Enter in Finder opens the file”: Enter launches files and opens folders, `⇧Enter` renames. “New” in the Finder menu: right-click an empty spot → “New” → a text document, Markdown, an empty file, documents, spreadsheets and presentations (for installed office apps) and images (PNG, JPEG, SVG always; PSD, XCF, KRA if a suitable app is installed; size 1920×1080, 1024×1024, 512×512 or custom). The same menu opens with `⌃⌥N` and from the menu-bar and Dock icon menu. Finder shows no extensions inside iCloud Drive.
- **Take a break.** Games: Tetris, Solitaire, Durak, JezzBall, Lode Runner, Snake, Arcan, Diam and Pacman. Games with scores keep a high-score table.
- **Calculator** in the style of the old CalcNew: two memory cells, powers, roots, factorial, percent like an ordinary calculator, keyboard input. The “Converter” tab: a quick-entry line plus length, mass, volume, temperature, speed, time, data, area and about twenty currencies (rates refresh at most once an hour; offline, the last saved ones are used).
- **File manager.** Two panes in the spirit of Total Commander: copy (`F5`), move (`F6`), new folder (`F7`), Trash (`F8`), view (`F3`), edit (`F4`), rename (`F2`, including batch rename with masks). Files can be dragged between panes and into other apps; each pane has a search, and when a search is open the commands act on the selected result. Android phones (“File transfer” mode) open right in a pane over USB: copy, rename and delete are available.
- **Junk cleanup.** App caches, logs, system caches, the Trash, Xcode data, installers in Downloads, leftovers of deleted apps (only your own files; files owned by others, such as VPN client services, are left alone). Any category can be expanded to pick individual items. Removed items go to the Trash, where you can get them back (except system files and the contents of the Trash itself).
- **Big and old files.** Search by size and age, the selection goes to the Trash.
- **Maintenance.** Free memory, flush the DNS cache, speed up Mail, re-index Spotlight, repair permissions, thin Time Machine snapshots, rebuild the “Open With” list, clear the Quick Look cache, restart Dock and Finder, the audio service or Bluetooth. Each task shows exactly what it will do; nothing is selected in advance.
- **Smart clipboard** (off by default). A history of 3, 5 or 10 copies, `⇧⌘V`, pinned entries. Passwords are not saved. Remembering the history between launches is a separate setting, off by default.
- **UnRAR, UnZip, 7z, GZ…** Extracts RAR, 7z, ZIP, TAR, GZ, BZ2, XZ, ISO and other archives, including password-protected and multi-volume ones. Creates 7z, ZIP, TAR.GZ, TAR.BZ2 and TAR.XZ; 7z and ZIP support a password and splitting into volumes. A ZIP password can only contain Latin letters, digits and symbols. The needed part (7-Zip) is downloaded on first use if necessary.
- **Duplicates and similar photos.** Exact copies are found by content, similar pictures by a reduced fingerprint of the image. Only what you tick goes to the Trash, you can tick any number of copies, and before deleting you see a warning if some group would be left with none. Folders can be excluded from the results (a tree with checkboxes or a button in a file row), and your choice is remembered.
- **Disk analyzer** in the style of DaisyDisk: a pie chart of used space, folder navigation, moving to the Trash. When reopened, only changed folders are rescanned.
- **Download and convert.** A large window with four sections:
  - *YouTube download:* paste a link, choose the quality; the file goes to Downloads. A “What if it fails?” button explains what to do if YouTube refuses.
  - *Audio converter:* any audio or video to MP3, M4A, FLAC, WAV, AIFF, OGG or Opus.
  - *Text and documents:* TXT, RTF, HTML, DOC, DOCX, ODT, Markdown, PDF, FB2 and EPUB into each other; pictures and scanned PDFs become text with the built-in macOS recognition, without the internet.
  - *Video converter:* MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI or GIF; codec, quality, resolution (as in the original, 4K, 1080p, 720p, 480p, 360p or custom), sound mode, trimming. Results of all converters are saved to Downloads.
  - Every section has a “Clear window” button. Helper programs (ffmpeg and yt-dlp) are downloaded once on first use and can be updated with a button.
- **External drives.** Format flash drives as FAT32, FAT16, exFAT, Mac OS Extended or APFS. The “Speed” tab measures reading and writing and compares the result with typical devices.
- **Task manager.** Processes, a load log, details, performance graphs, services and startup items. You can end and suspend processes, change priority and set automatic rules based on CPU load.

### Windows, keyboard and layout

- **“Minimize all”.** A menu-bar button and the `⌃⌥M` hotkey, plus restoring windows when the screen is empty. Needs the “Accessibility” permission.
- **New clean desktop** (off by default). The “+” button on the top bar creates a separate desktop with its own icons; your main desktop and iCloud are untouched.
- **Automatic layout switcher** (off by default). Fixes a word typed in the wrong layout; leaves website addresses alone; apps can be excluded. The `⌃⌥Space` hotkey fixes the last word or the selected text. Typed text is not saved.
- **Your own layout key.** Globe/fn or your own shortcut switches the layout.
- **Layout flag.** A small indicator of the current layout with a flag, on screen and in the menu bar.
- **Start icon.** Three modes in Settings: the regular Dock icon, a button next to the Dock, or a button on screen above all windows. Size and distance from the Dock are adjustable, and the icon background (color, transparency, saturation, outline; a live preview is shown in Settings) can be changed; the button magnifies with the Dock icons when Dock magnification is on; on macOS 26 and later the button is Liquid Glass. The picture on the icon can be swapped for one of eight and recolored; magnification with the Dock can be switched off.
- **Windows on Dock hover.** Window thumbnails of an app above its Dock icon.
- **`⌥Tab` window switcher.** Window thumbnails, search and window buttons.
- **Windows from the keyboard.** `⌃⌥` and the arrows put a window on half the screen, on the full screen, or minimize it.
- **`⌘X` in Finder.** “Cut” files; the next `⌘V` paste moves them.

### Settings, updates and permissions

- **Settings.** Language (system, Russian, English), theme, hotkeys, gesture, launch at login, pinning in the Dock, the “About” tab.
- **Updates.** Every 6 hours (and after your Mac wakes up) the app checks whether a new version is out; if so, Settings shows a message and an “Update” button. You can also check manually with “Check for updates…” in the app menu.
- **Permissions.** The “Permissions” tab explains why each permission is needed and opens the right pane of System Settings: “Full Disk Access”, “Accessibility”, “Screen & System Audio Recording” (only for window thumbnails), “App Management” and “Files & Folders”. The app works without them, but some features will be unavailable.

## Privacy and security

- No telemetry and no accounts. The app goes online only for the icons of websites you added, for currency rates when you ask to convert currency, for updates and, while you use download and convert, for helper programs (ffmpeg, yt-dlp, 7-Zip) and for the links you pasted yourself.
- The administrator password is entered in the app window, is never saved, and is kept in memory for no more than five minutes.
- Everything you type into search and other fields stays on your Mac. Settings are stored in `~/Library/Application Support/StartMenu`.
